Buyer's Guide

How to Select the Perfect Jupiter Stone

Selecting an authentic Yellow Sapphire requires both knowledge and intuition. The most prized specimens come from Sri Lanka (Ceylon), Madagascar, and Thailand, with Ceylon stones commanding the highest regard for their exceptional clarity and naturally vivid yellow colour unenhanced by heat treatment.

When evaluating a Pukhraj, consider the four pillars of quality: colour, clarity, cut, and carat weight. The ideal colour is a pure, medium-strong yellow — neither too pale nor veering into orange or green. Clarity should be high; while minor inclusions are natural, avoid stones with visible fractures or heavy cloudiness that mute the stone's brilliance.

  • Prefer natural, untreated stones — heat treatment diminishes astrological potency according to Vedic gemology.
  • Request a reputable laboratory certificate (e.g., GIA, IGI, GRS) confirming origin and treatment status.
  • A minimum weight of 2–3 carats is generally recommended for astrological purposes, though quality always supersedes size.
  • The cut should maximise brilliance — excellent light reflection signals a high-quality stone.
  • Always consult a qualified Vedic astrologer to confirm Jupiter's placement in your birth chart before purchase.
